01

Project overview

Making movement part of the game—not a metric beside it.

Step By Step asks a simple product question: what if progress in a mobile story depended on progress away from the screen?

The app turns real activity into a game resource. HealthKit supplies step and walking-distance data, a conversion system translates steps into energy, and story decisions consume that energy alongside health. The result is one connected loop rather than separate fitness and storytelling features.

Project origin

Step By Step expands the original Survive project into a deeper system with fitness data, persistent progress, achievements, checkpoints, and replayable outcomes.

Project purpose
Encourage everyday movement by making physical activity useful inside a narrative game.
My role
Independent product designer, iOS developer, systems designer, and story creator.
Technology
Swift, SwiftUI, HealthKit, Swift Charts, MVVM, Combine, and UserDefaults.
Fitness system
Daily goals, step and distance history, streaks, seven-day averages, trend comparisons, and achievements.
Story system
Five playable days, 28 chapter records, energy and health costs, saved progress, attempts, one completion ending, and a replayable failure state.
Privacy approach
The app requests read access for steps and walking or running distance; project progress is stored on the device.

02

The core loop

Walk. Convert. Decide.

  1. 01
    ●

    Move in the real world

    HealthKit brings daily steps and walking distance into the app while progress is measured against a personal step goal.

  2. 02
    →

    Turn steps into energy

    The daily goal is divided into ten energy segments. Completed segments can be converted into points for the player.

  3. 03
    ◆

    Spend energy on choices

    Story decisions carry energy and health consequences, linking activity directly to survival and progression.

Example conversion

10,000-step goal ÷ 10 = 1,000 steps per energy

The conversion scales with the player’s chosen daily goal, so the system rewards consistent progress instead of one fixed number.

05

Building the product

Turning two data worlds into one experience.

The central design challenge is keeping fitness data, player state, and branching story progress independent enough to test and evolve—while making them feel inseparable to the player.

  • Swift
  • SwiftUI
  • HealthKit
  • Swift Charts
  • MVVM
  • Combine
  1. 01

    Make activity part of the core mechanic.

    The fitness layer has meaning because it changes what the player can do, not simply what the dashboard displays.

  2. 02

    Keep HealthKit behind focused boundaries.

    Health data retrieval, step calculations, achievements, and story state live in separate models and view models.

  3. 03

    Translate history into useful signals.

    Best days, streaks, averages, trends, and milestones make raw activity data easier to understand.

  4. 04

    Design persistence around returning players.

    Saved chapters, health, energy, goals, attempts, and earned achievements support short sessions across multiple days.
